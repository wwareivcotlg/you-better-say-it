How to Play

  1. Split into two teams: Team Purple and Team Gold. One player holds the phone.
  2. Describe the word on screen to your team — but never say the word itself (or a piece of it)!
  3. When your team guesses it, tap GOT IT and pass the phone to the other team fast.
  4. The ticking speeds up as time runs out. The timer length is a secret — stay on your toes!
  5. When the buzzer sounds, the team holding the phone loses the round. The other team scores.
  6. First team to 8 points wins — eight, the number of new beginnings!
